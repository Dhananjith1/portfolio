# ColdPulse — Last-Mile Cold-Chain Monitoring

<small>Classification: Project-management simulation · Role: Scrum Delivery Planning · Focus: Offline-first monitoring concept</small>

## Overview

ColdPulse is a simulated last-mile cold-chain monitoring concept for medicine transport. The scenario considers how a delivery team could identify temperature excursions when vehicles travel through areas with intermittent power or network connectivity.

The exercise concerns planning and risk governance. It is not a medical device, a validated cold-chain product, or a live medicine-monitoring service.

## Objective

Practise defining a small offline-first MVP concept that records temperature observations, handles connectivity gaps, and makes exceptions visible for appropriate human review.

## Planning focus

- Used **2°C to 8°C** as the scenario's target storage band for the medicine cold-chain example.
- Considered offline capture so that a temporary network interruption would not automatically erase a reading.
- Framed exception alerts for review by a responsible pharmacist or authorised staff member.
- Considered risks around sensor accuracy, missing observations, delayed synchronisation, battery or power loss, and incorrect alert handling.
- Used Scrum-style scope and backlog planning, with Jira referenced as the project-management tool.

## Project-management work

The case study focuses on MVP scope, stakeholder responsibilities, backlog prioritisation, exception-handling requirements, risk ownership, and acceptance criteria for data completeness and alert visibility.

## Limitations

The 2°C–8°C range is a scenario requirement, not evidence of a validated storage protocol for every medicine. No sensor network, transport deployment, real medicine data, clinical review workflow, or regulatory validation is claimed. The concept should not be used to make treatment or medicine-handling decisions.
