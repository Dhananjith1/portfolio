# AegisLogix — AI Supply Chain Platform

<small>Classification: Project-planning simulation · Role: Project Planning Lead · Planned work: 63 story points</small>

## Overview

AegisLogix is a simulated logistics and supply-chain control-tower concept. The planning exercise considers how telemetry from supply-chain operations might be brought together so that operational teams can see events and coordinate responses.

The case study is about organising a hypothetical delivery effort. It does not claim that a production logistics platform, AI model, IoT fleet, or Kafka pipeline was built or deployed.

## Objective

Practise translating a broad product idea into a structured delivery scope, squad responsibilities, backlog, dependencies, and risks.

## Planning approach

The simulation organises the work around three cross-functional squads:

- **IoT:** the conceptual device and telemetry stream.
- **AI:** the proposed analytical and alerting capability.
- **Frontend:** the user-facing control-tower experience.

An event-driven architecture using Apache Kafka is referenced as a design concept for the hypothetical telemetry flow. It is not presented as an implemented integration.

## Project-management work

- Framed the product concept and planning boundaries.
- Organised the scope into workstreams and backlog items.
- Coordinated dependencies between the IoT, AI, and frontend workstreams.
- Modelled a 63-story-point plan in ClickUp.
- Identified integration, data quality, delivery sequencing, and cross-squad dependency risks.

## Tools and artefacts

**Tool referenced:** ClickUp.

**Planning artefacts:** product-requirement outline, high-level architecture assumptions, squad responsibilities, backlog and story-point plan, dependency view, and risk/issue considerations.

The full ClickUp workspace and any separate product-design files are not included in this repository.

## Limitations

The 63 story points represent planned work in a simulation, not completed engineering output or measured team velocity. There is no production deployment, live carrier integration, device telemetry, or validated AI performance to report.
