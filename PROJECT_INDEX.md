## Accuracy notes

- Story points, service levels, latency values, and time-saving figures in the simulation documents are planning estimates, targets, or modelled scenarios—not measured production results.
- The telecom incident records are synthetic, not data from Dialog, SLT-Mobitel, or another operator.
- DIMO AgriCare and the CLAAS harvester scenario are used only as a simulation context; this document does not imply endorsement, partnership, or real field-service delivery.
- AeroHive describes a high-level planning exercise. It does not claim physical drone deployment, operational flight testing, or disaster-response deployment.
- ColdPulse is a planning concept, not a validated medical device or clinical service.
